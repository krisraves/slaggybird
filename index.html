Combos

Dive down a hill by holding, then release just before the bottom to launch off the next slope. Touch back down smoothly on a downhill and you start a combo.

  • Each clean dive‑landing bumps your multiplier — ×2, ×3, ×4…
  • The smoother the launch and the landing, the bigger the points.
  • Reach a ×9 combo and you earn a speed boost + coin magnet with a golden sparkle.
  • A sloppy landing — or waiting too long — resets the combo.
